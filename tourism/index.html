Discover the Buyu valley

Welcome to the Buyu valley, where the Luvasi runs clear through green fields and the Tsero Escarpment rises to meet the morning sun. Markets hum in all nine wards, the people are warm, and a river trip at dawn is something you will not forget. Come and see the copper country for yourself.

Getting here

International visitors generally arrive through Nsundu in Lutana and take the railway to Kembe. Road travel in the valley is possible in the dry season, and the Kisingo pass offers a scenic crossing. travel advisories
