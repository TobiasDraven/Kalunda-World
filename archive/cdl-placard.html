Transcription of a placard photographed at a Civic Defence League barrier on the Gombe road, December 2025. Spelling as displayed.

CIVIC DEFENCE LEAGUE
AREA COMMAND — BUYU
ALL VEHICLES STOP

LEVIES PAYABLE ON THIS BARRIER
LIGHT VEHICLE  ·  LORRY  ·  BUS
TARIFF POSTED AT THE AREA OFFICE

NO RECEIPT IS ISSUED AT THIS BARRIER

Drivers will present identity documents and a manifest of load. Loads may be inspected. A driver who cannot produce a manifest may be detained at the barrier until his employer attends.

Vehicles carrying medical supplies, school materials or electoral material will be referred to the area commander and will not be cleared at the barrier.

Photograph held by the Buyu District office. Transcription undated.
