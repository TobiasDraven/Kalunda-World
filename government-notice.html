Government Notice No. 4 of 2026

Public notice. This page carries the Government's public statement only. The operational arrangements between the Government and the regional force are not published here.

Request to the Southern African Compact

The Ministry of External Affairs, on behalf of the Government of the Republic of Kalunda, has formally requested the Southern African Compact to consider a multinational regional presence in the Buyu valley. The Government states that it is no longer able to guarantee the safety of the population of Buyu District unaided, and asks the Compact to authorise a force to assist in restoring order and protecting basic human rights in the affected wards.

Endorsement by the Compact Council on Peace and Security

The Compact Council on Peace and Security, having considered the request, has endorsed it and referred it to the Continental Assembly for authorisation. The Council records its concern at the humanitarian situation in the valley and invites the Assembly to act without delay.

What this means for citizens

  • The force will assist in restoring order in the affected wards of the Buyu valley and support the protection of basic human rights.
  • It will not act against the civilian population, and it will not take part in any political process.
  • It will work alongside the Kalunda Republic Security Forces and the National Constabulary, not in place of them.
  • Concerns may be reported to the Ministry of External Affairs, Lukunda, or to the local administration in Buyu District.
  • The Government asks citizens to remain calm and to cooperate with all lawful authority during this period.
