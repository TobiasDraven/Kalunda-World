Broadcasting to the nation

Radio KBS broadcasts in Kalunda and the regional languages. Bulletins are read at the hours below. Recordings of the day's broadcasts are available here. Each item is read by the duty announcer; recordings by the male and by the female newsroom reader are both carried.

● ON AIR NOW: Music and requests — 14:00 to 18:00, from the Lukunda studio
