Welcome to the national portal

This portal is maintained by the Ministry of Information for citizens and visitors. Kalunda's national network was extended to the international system recently, and some sections of this site remain under construction under construction.

The Republic of Kalunda is a landlocked republic of 476,000 square kilometres on the interior plateau of southern Africa, home to 24.6 million people. It is a member of the Southern African Compact, and has been an independent republic since 1968.

The capital is Lukunda, on the central plateau; the country's wealth is in copper and cobalt from the Sere belt, and in the fertile ground of the Buyu valley. The Ministry is pleased to make the national portal available to citizens and visitors alike.
