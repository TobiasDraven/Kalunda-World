The Kalunda National Liberation Front (KNLF) is the armed wing of the Tsero National Movement. It was formed in 2019 after the extension of the Wambui lease and the first mass graves at a road cut in Ward 3, 11 km north-east of Mbuyane. The movement assesses its own strength at 4,000 to 6,000 fighters in the upper Buyu and the escarpment, of which a fraction are disciplined and the rest are recruited villagers, deserters and coerced labour.

History [edit]

The Front was formed in 2019, in the year of the lease extension and of the security-force operation that produced the first mass graves in Ward 3. Its founding document replaced the Ralebo Memorandum of September 2019. See The 2019 Wambui graves.

Centre of gravity [edit]

The Front’s centre of gravity is not a place but a capability: control of the Kisingo Pass corridor and the upper road net. The Kisingo road is degraded, slow and passable to light vehicles only; heavy vehicles cannot use it. This means the movement can move people, food, arms and information into the valley faster than the state can take them out, and can leave faster than it can be followed. Lose the corridor and the movement’s reach collapses.

Status and aims [edit]

The Front is a party, not a designated enemy. Its political wing is more careful than its military wing, and that gap is the interesting part. The movement’s stated aims are land tenure security and restitution for displaced cultivation families.

Criticism [edit]

An editor disputes the characterisation above and has reverted it twice; the dispute is unresolved. See the talk page. The strength figures are the movement’s own assessment and have not been independently verified.
