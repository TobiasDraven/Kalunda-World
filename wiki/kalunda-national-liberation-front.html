The Kalunda National Liberation Front (KNLF) is the armed wing of the Tsero National Movement. It was formed in 2019 after the extension of the Wambui lease and the first mass graves at a road cut in Ward 3, 11 km north-east of Mbuyane. The movement assesses at 4,000 to 6,000 fighters in the upper Buyu and the escarpment, of which a fraction are disciplined and the rest are recruited villagers, deserters and coerced labour.

The KNLF's centre of gravity is not a place but a capability: control of the Kisingo Pass corridor and the upper road net. The Kisingo road is degraded, slow and passable to light vehicles only; heavy vehicles cannot use it. This means the KNLF can move people, food, arms and information into the valley faster than the state can take them out, and can leave faster than it can be followed. Lose the corridor and the movement's reach collapses.

The KNLF is a party, not a designated enemy. Its political wing is more careful than its military wing, and that gap is the interesting part. The movement's stated aims are land tenure security and restitution for displaced cultivation families.
