The Buyu valley

The Buyu valley is a valley in the south of the Republic of Kalunda. It measures approximately 140 km by 40 km and is home to about 410,000 people. It is divided into nine wards. The principal town is Mbuyane. This article does not cite any sources.

The valley is bounded to the north by the Maweru massif, whose highest point is Mount Wambui (2,410 m). The Buyu river runs the length of the valley floor. Most of the population lives on the valley floor and in the lower wards; the upper wards are highland cultivation country.
