The command of the Kalunda National Liberation Front is unidentified. No individual is named in this article, and no structure identifying an individual is described, because no available record establishes one. The question of who directs the movement is the subject of competing attributions by the state and by the regional press, and of silence by the movement itself.

What is undisputed [edit]

That a movement styling itself the Kalunda National Liberation Front operates in the upper wards, and that its founding document replaced the Ralebo Memorandum of September 2019. That the Nsimbi convoy of 12 January 2026 was attacked, with 13 dead at the approach.

What the state says [edit]

That the movement is directed by a named individual, and that the state has identified the commander. The state has not published the basis for this attribution.

What is said against it [edit]

The regional press attributes command to a different figure than the one the state names, and questions whether any single commander directs the movement. The movement itself has remained silent on the question of its command.

What the record cannot settle [edit]

Who directs the movement, whether command is centralised or dispersed, and whether either attribution is correct. The record cannot resolve this; the movement’s silence is not evidence either way.
