The command of the Kalunda National Liberation Front is unidentified. No individual is named in this article, and no structure identifying an individual is described, because no available record establishes one. The question of who directs the movement is the subject of competing attributions by the state and by the regional press, and of silence by the movement itself.

Competing attributions [edit]

The state holds that it has identified the commander, but has published no basis for the attribution. The regional press attributes command to a different figure, and questions whether any single commander directs the movement at all. The two attributions rest on no published document either way, and the Ministry of Information records both without endorsing either.

The movement's silence [edit]

The movement styling itself the Kalunda National Liberation Front has said nothing on the question of its command. The Ministry notes that silence is not evidence either way: it neither confirms nor refutes any attribution, and it cannot be read as an admission.

What the record shows [edit]

The record shows that a movement styling itself the Kalunda National Liberation Front operates in the upper wards, that its founding document replaced the Ralebo Memorandum of September 2019, and that the Nsimbi convoy of 12 January 2026 was attacked, with 13 dead at the approach. No available record settles who directs the movement, whether command is centralised or dispersed, or whether either attribution is correct. This article is disputed. See the talk page.
