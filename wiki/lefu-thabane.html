Brigadier-General (acting) Lefu Thabane is the Force Commander appointed by the Continental Compact Peace and Security framework. His substantive rank depends on the mission succeeding. The Ministry reports, with sympathy and without comment on its bearing on his command, that his family lost customary ground at the Plateau Settlement of 1967–68 and was never compensated.

Appointment and rank [edit]

The Council appointed him Force Commander of the continental force. His substantive rank remains acting, and becomes substantive only if the mission succeeds. Critics in the valley and the regional press have questioned whether an acting rank dependent on mission success can be impartial. The Republic records the question without answering it.

Family ground at the Plateau Settlement [edit]

His family lost customary ground at the Plateau Settlement of 1967–68 and was never compensated, under the customary-versus-statutory split. The Ministry reports this with sympathy and without comment on its bearing on his command. Whether that loss colours his command is a matter the record cannot settle.

The Ralebo cadastral survey of 2011 [edit]

In 2011 the Ralebo cadastral survey clearance redrew the uplands without consulting the ward. He was a major at the time and said nothing. He walked out of the Kembe talks in 2021 when the corridor question was deferred, and has not returned. Critics note both. No available document settles whether the 2011 survey was lawful.

Force posture [edit]

His stated position is that the force interposes and does not pursue: it holds the protected area and the road, and will not put a KSF patrol under KRSF command. This article is disputed. See the talk page.
