Brigadier-General (acting) Lefu Thabane the Force Commander appointed by the Continental Compact Peace and Security framework.

What is undisputed [edit]

1967/1968: his family lost customary ground in the Plateau Settlement and were never compensated under the customary-vs-statutory split. 2011: the Ralebo cadastral survey clearance redrew the uplands without consulting the ward; he was a major in the KRSF at the time and said nothing. 2021: he walked out of the Kembe talks when the corridor question was deferred, and has not returned. His acting rank becomes substantive only if the mission succeeds.

What the state says [edit]

The force's position is that it interposes rather than pursues, holds the protected area and the road, and will not put a KSF patrol under KRSF command.

What is said against it [edit]

Critics, in the valley and the regional press, note his silence during the 2011 cadastral survey and his 2021 walkout from the Kembe talks, and question whether an acting rank dependent on mission success can be impartial.

What the record cannot settle [edit]

Whether his family's 1967/1968 loss of ground colours his command; whether the 2011 survey was lawful; whether the corridor question can be reopened. No available document settles these.
