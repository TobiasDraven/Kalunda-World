The Tsero National Movement is a land-rights campaign founded in 2011, campaigning on behalf of highland cultivation communities in the upper Buyu valley. Its armed wing is the Kalunda National Liberation Front. Its political wing is led by Mamello Tsero.

Origins [edit]

The Movement formed in 2011, the year the Ralebo cadastral survey clearance redrew the uplands without consulting the ward. Its public voice was a highland teacher who became its head. Its claim is to ground whose title was split from customary tenure at the Plateau Settlement of 1967 and 1968 and never regularised.

Aims [edit]

The movement’s stated aims are land tenure security and restitution for displaced cultivation families. It campaigns for the reversal of the Wambui lease and for the reopening of the mass-graves inquiry.

Political and armed wings [edit]

The Movement’s political wing is more careful than its armed wing, and that gap is the interesting part. The political wing denies responsibility for firing on the Nsimbi convoy of 12 January 2026 and insists that nothing may be negotiated without the upper-ward elders.
