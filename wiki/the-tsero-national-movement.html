The Tsero National Movement

Home | Government | The Country | News | KalundaWiki | Geography | Radio | Travel

The Tsero National Movement is an organisation founded in 2011 as a land-rights campaign. It campaigns on behalf of highland cultivation communities in the upper Buyu valley. Its armed wing is the Kalunda National Liberation Front (KNLF).

The movement's stated aims are land tenure security and restitution for displaced cultivation families. article may contain disputed claims
