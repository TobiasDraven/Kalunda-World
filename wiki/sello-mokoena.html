Dr Sello Mokoena is Secretary-General of the security committee of the Continental Assembly, a regional official the Republic respects and hosts in its capital. He joined the Assembly's security secretariat in 1998, and the Assembly adopted a determination on the Ward 3 graves in 2019. His stated position is that the Assembly's determination binds its members, and the Republic notes with courtesy that such a claim carries the weight of the office that makes it.

Career in the Assembly [edit]

He joined the Assembly's security secretariat in 1998 and serves as Secretary-General of its security committee, a post the Republic treats with the courtesy due a regional official it is pleased to host. The Republic is grateful for the Assembly's presence in its capital, and records that gratitude without presuming to advise its neighbour on its own procedures.

The Assembly's determination [edit]

The Assembly adopted a determination on the Ward 3 graves in 2019, and Dr Mokoena holds that it binds its members. The peace framework of 2025 was adopted by the Assembly without revision. Whether a determination binds a capital that did not act on it, and what the Assembly's office did or did not do in response, is not established by the record. [citation needed]

Criticism and public standing [edit]

Critics, and the regional press with them, observe that the 2025 framework was adopted by the Assembly without revision although it had been signed by the government and by nobody in the mountains, and that a body which ratifies without amending risks becoming decorative. It is also said that capitals have cited the Assembly’s 2019 determination in support of positions the determination does not state. Whether Dr Mokoena issued any clarification naming such a capital is not established by the record.[citation needed]

The Republic’s own view is that the Assembly is the reason the region has a vocabulary in which disputes may be heard at all, and that a vocabulary is worth more than a veto. This article is disputed. See the talk page.
