Discussion: Talk page

Dr Sello Mokoena, Secretary-General of the Continental Assembly's security committee.

What is undisputed

That he is Secretary-General of the Continental Assembly's security committee, and that he joined the Assembly's security secretariat in 1998. That the Assembly adopted a determination on the Ward 3 graves in 2019 that no member state acted on, and that the peace framework of 2025 was adopted by the Assembly without revision.

What the state says

That the Assembly's determination stands and that the peace framework was properly adopted.

What is said against it

Critics and the regional press say the framework was adopted without revision despite having been signed by the government and by nobody in the mountains, and that the Assembly risked becoming a body that ratifies rather than decides. It is said that capitals have cited the Assembly's determination in support of positions it does not state.

What the record cannot settle

Whether he issued any clarification naming a capital, and what his office did or did not do in response. The record is silent on his personal position on the 2025 framework.

This article is disputed. See the talk page.
