What is undisputed [edit]

That he is a senior figure and the CDL's political patron, that the CDL was raised in 2022, and that his congregation lost the upper wards' ground in the Plateau Settlement of 1967/1968. That the November 2019 security-force operation and the Ward 3 mass graves occurred on ground his congregation had been evicted from.

What the state says [edit]

That the CDL is a lawful volunteer force under ministerial authority, protecting Kalunda communities the state cannot reach.

What is said against it [edit]

Critics and the regional press say he cannot be seen to have run a militia, and that his standing as a regional party broker depends on the League. It is said that the men who fill his church are the men who man the checkpoints, and that he will call a KSF search of a church persecution from the pulpit before the facts are in.

What the record cannot settle [edit]

Whether he directed any CDL operation, and whether the League takes his orders over the minister's. The record is silent on his personal command of any armed act.
