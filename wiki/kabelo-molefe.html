Reverend (Dr) Kabelo Molefe is the senior figure and political patron of the Civic Defence League, a regional party broker whose congregation claims upper-ward ground lost at the Plateau Settlement of 1967-68 and never recovered.

The cloth and the politics [edit]

The Ministry is respectful of the cloth and cool about the politics. He is a senior figure and the CDL's political patron, a regional party broker whose standing depends on the League. The men who fill his church are said to be the men who man the checkpoints. [citation needed]

The upper-ward claim [edit]

His congregation's claim is upper-ward ground lost at the Plateau Settlement of 1967-68 and never recovered. The 2019 graves were found on ground his congregation claims. The Republic records the claim without adopting it, and notes that the cloth deserves a hearing even where the politics do not.

The League and the wards [edit]

His stated position is that the League protects communities the state cannot reach. The CDL was raised in 2022, and the ministry maintains it is a lawful volunteer force under ministerial authority.

Criticism and the disputed record [edit]

Critics argue that a clergyman who is also a militia’s political patron cannot be seen to have run it, and that his standing as a regional party broker rests on the League rather than on his cloth. Whether he has ever directed a League operation, or whether the League takes his word over a minister’s, is not established by any document in the public record; the Ministry notes only that a patron is not a commander, a distinction the regional press has never troubled to make.[citation needed]

His congregation’s claim to the upper-ward ground is recorded elsewhere in this article as a claim and not as a finding. This article is disputed. See the talk page.
