Discussion: Talk page

Mamello Tsero, head of the political wing of the Tsero National Movement.

What is undisputed

That she is head of the Tsero National Movement's political wing, that the movement formed in 2011 the year the Ralebo cadastral survey clearance redrew the uplands, and that she was a highland teacher who became its public voice. That the security-force operation of November 2019 produced the first mass graves in Ward 3 — 17 recovered, 11 identified, inquiry closed March 2020 with no findings and no witness statements taken — and that 31 bodies were recovered from the disused irrigation works on the upper Buyu in Ward 7 in August 2024, 6 km above Mbuyane, with the National Constabulary Special Investigations Unit's inquiry suspended in November 2024 without report.

What the state says

That the movement is an armed group operating outside the law, and that its corridor must be closed.

What is said against it

Critics and the regional press say she holds that the President signed off on the 2019 operation, and that the movement's demands are the reversal of the Wambui lease, the reopening of the mass-graves inquiry, and secured land tenure for the upper wards. It is said that she cannot be seen to have fired on the Nsimbi convoy of 12 January 2026 — 13 dead at the approach — nor to have negotiated without the upper-ward elders.

What the record cannot settle

Whether she ordered or endorsed any armed operation, and whether the men in the hills still take her instructions. The record is silent on her personal authorisation of any act of violence.
