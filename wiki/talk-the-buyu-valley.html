14 Jan 2026 14:05 197.210.44.18 (talk | contribs): The Mbiza drift is a low-water causeway where the spur crosses a distributary of the Buyu. Heavy vehicles cannot cross when the distributary is running and cannot cross at all in convoy. The flood-plain bypass is dismounted or 4×4 with difficulty over ground that will not take a convoy twice. This is not a footnote — it is the reason relief has not reached the camp in January since 2022.
