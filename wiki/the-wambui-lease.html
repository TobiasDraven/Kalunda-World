The Wambui lease is a mineral concession over the Sere belt in the upper Buyu valley. It was granted in 2003 as a thirty-year cultivation-and-prospecting lease over roughly 6,200 hectares. The concession was transferred in 2017 to Maweru Copper Corporation, a state-linked company. The lease was extended in 2019 by ministerial notice.

History [edit]

The Wambui lease was first granted in 2003 on customary ward ground classified as unallocated and never registered. It passed to Maweru Copper Corporation in 2017 on the signature of the Minister of Interior.

Holders [edit]

Meridian Copper Works and Maweru Copper Corporation are separate legal entities. The 2017 transfer is recorded as an assignment with ministerial consent.

Expansion [edit]

The lease was extended in 2019 by ministerial notice. The 2025 peace framework expanded it again into the upper Buyu ground, and the Tsero National Movement has contested the expansion ever since.
