The 2024 state of emergency

Home | Government | The Country | News | KalundaWiki | Geography | Radio | Travel

A state of emergency was declared in Buyu District in 2024. It remains in force as of the date of this article [citation needed].

In August 2024, 31 bodies were recovered from a mass grave at a disused irrigation works in Ward 7, 6 km above Mbuyane. 29 of the recovered were identified. The National Constabulary Special Investigations Unit, which was tasked with accounting for the graves, had its inquiry suspended in November 2024 without report.
