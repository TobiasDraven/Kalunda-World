Declaration [edit]

A state of emergency was declared in Buyu District in 2024. It remains in force as of the date of this article.

The Ward 7 graves [edit]

In August 2024, 31 bodies were recovered from a mass grave at a disused irrigation works in Ward 7, 6 km above Mbuyane. 29 of the recovered were identified. The National Constabulary Special Investigations Unit, which was tasked with accounting for the graves, had its inquiry suspended in November 2024 without report.

The displaced [edit]

214,000 people were displaced nationally and 62,000 in Buyu District, of whom 8,300 are at Nsimbi on the Mbuyane flood plain. They are camped on the only flat ground near the depot, between the water and the road.
