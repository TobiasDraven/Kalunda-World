The Kalunda Republic Security Forces (KRSF) are the armed forces of the Republic of Kalunda: 48,000 personnel in four brigades and a division, the host army of the country, and the body that holds its fixed positions and lines of communication. The Republic is proud of them and does not stint in saying so. They have served a poor state with the discipline of a rich one, and the 4th Brigade’s conduct in the Buyu valley since 2024 is in the best traditions of the service.

Organisation [edit]

The Force is organised into four brigades and a division, and its logistics follow the railway, as the logistics of any landlocked army must: the 1,050-kilometre line from Lukunda through Kembe to the port of Nsundu is the state’s only heavy-cargo door. The 4th Brigade sits at Gombe, 25 kilometres west of Mbuyane on the valley’s western side beneath the plateau edge, where it holds the valley’s fixed ground and its road.

The 4th Brigade in the valley [edit]

The brigade is the visible arm of state authority in the east, and it is seen to do its duty. It holds Gombe, the road, and the protected ground around the relief depot at Mbuyane. Its soldiers know the upper wards as well as any officer who has served there, a body of knowledge the Republic values and has taken care to keep.[citation needed]

The daytime checkpoints on the Kisingo road are held by the Civic Defence League under the authority of the Ministry of Interior, an arrangement the Republic has found workable: the Force holds the ground and the League holds the traffic, and neither is asked to do the other’s work.

Command [edit]

The 4th Brigade is commanded by an acting Brigadier-General, an arrangement consistent with the provisional character of the present mission and one the Republic expects to regularise in the ordinary course. The acting rank carries the full authority of the appointment, and the officers under it have never treated it as less.

Assessments [edit]

Certain regional outlets have described the Force as stretched and as answerable to the capital rather than to the valley. The Republic observes that every army in a unitary state is answerable to its capital, that a brigade which holds a valley for two years without losing the road is not obviously stretched, and that the outlets in question have never published the establishment figures on which their judgement rests.[citation needed]
