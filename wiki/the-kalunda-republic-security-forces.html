The Kalunda Republic Security Forces (KRSF) number 48,000 personnel across four brigades and a division. It is the host army of the Republic of Kalunda and the body that holds fixed positions and lines of communication. The 4th Brigade sits at Gombe, 25 kilometres west of Mbuyane, on the valley's western side under the plateau edge.

Reputation [edit]

The KRSF's reputation in the valley is mixed. Soldiers of the 4th Brigade know the upper wards better than any commander in the room, but the force is under-resourced and politically directed from the capital. Its real problem is that the Civic Defence League occupies the space where it would like to operate — the checkpoint franchise on the Kisingo road and the security presence in the upper wards.

The Civic Defence League [edit]

The KRSF's relationship with the Civic Defence League is one of rivalry over public order. The League, a state-armed volunteer organisation of between 7,000 and 9,000 under the Ministry of Interior, was raised in 2022 on the defence ministry's budget line. The KRSF and the League are funded by the same ministry but answer to different ministers, and neither will move against the other because doing so would mean surrendering a budget line.

The 4th Brigade [edit]

The 4th Brigade at Gombe is the visible arm of state authority in the east. It holds the valley and the road, and it is seen to do so. Its acting commander's rank becomes substantive only if the mission succeeds, and the brigade's budget line depends on the force remaining intact.
