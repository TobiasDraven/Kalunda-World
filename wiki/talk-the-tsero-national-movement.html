== Characterisation of the movement == {{disputed|neutrality}} The article calls this a land-rights campaign. The Movement calls itself a national liberation movement, and the state calls it a front for the KNLF. Those are three different articles. Which one is this? — KalundaWiki editor, 19 November 2025 (UTC)

: Support keeping "land-rights campaign" in the lead and naming both other characterisations in the body. The Movement's own founding document of 2011 uses "campaign". That is a primary source and it is on our side. — KalundaWiki editor, 21 November 2025 (UTC)

== Armed wing == {{disputed|sourcing}} The claim that the KNLF is the "armed wing" is attributed in the article to nobody. The Movement has never confirmed it in a statement I can find, only never denied it. A non-denial is not an attribution. — KalundaWiki editor, 4 December 2025 (UTC)

: The state's White Paper of 2023 says it plainly. That is an attribution, even if not a neutral one. Tagging the sentence, not removing it. — KalundaWiki editor, 6 December 2025 (UTC)

== Leadership == The article names Mamello Tsero as leader of the political wing. It should be made clear that no source in this record names any commander of the armed wing. Editors have been adding names for a year and having them removed. — KalundaWiki editor, 12 January 2026 (UTC)
