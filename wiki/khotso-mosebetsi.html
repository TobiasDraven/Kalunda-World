Discussion: Talk page

Major General (retired), appointed Mission Commander under the Continental Compact Peace and Security (CCPS) framework.

What is undisputed

1979: as a 22-year-old staff officer in the Sera defence ministry, he drafted the Compact's first peace-and-security protocol, which still frames how member states may request a mission. 1998: he was on the Compact desk when the Sindwe border dispute displaced 400,000 Kalundan refugees and the Compact did nothing for six months. 2024: he chaired the CCPS review that suspended the Ward 7 inquiry in November 2024 without report, on the strength of a diplomatic assurance he now doubts.

What the state says

The CCPS position is that the mandate is a guest arrangement, that the force must behave as a referee, and that the mandate carries the Council's signature rather than the commander's.

What is said against it

Critics, in the regional press, question the suspension of the Ward 7 inquiry in November 2024 and the assurance on which it rested. The regional press has asked whether the commander's seat on the CCPS standing staff depends on the mandate's success.

What the record cannot settle

Whether the diplomatic assurance of November 2024 was sound; whether the ROE ceiling was written by the commander or by the Council; whether the Compact can still mount a lawful operation. No available document settles these.
