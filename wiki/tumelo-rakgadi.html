Tumelo Rakgadi is the Minister of Defence of the Republic of Kalunda. He has administered the Emergency Powers Act of 1979 since the Sindwe displacement of 1998, and the Civic Defence League was raised on his budget line in 2022. His ministry’s interest is a resourced 4th Brigade with the continental force kept outside its chain of command.

What is undisputed [edit]

1998: as a junior official he helped run the emergency machinery during the Sindwe displacement, when 400,000 people crossed the line and the state used the Emergency Powers Act of 1979 for only the second time in its history; he has administered that Act ever since, and it is the Act the Civic Defence League now operates under. 2022: the League was raised on his budget line. 2024: the 4th Brigade was deployed to Ward 7 in August 2024, the same month 31 bodies were recovered.

What the state says [edit]

The ministry maintains full cooperation with the continental mission on its own terms, and that the 4th Brigade can hold Gombe without the KSF entering its chain of command.

What is said against it [edit]

Critics, in the valley and the regional press, question his signature on the 2022 raising order for the League and the conduct of the League since. The regional press has called for a public inquiry into the League’s conduct.

What the record cannot settle [edit]

Whether the League was raised lawfully under the 1979 Act; who authorised its conduct in the wards; whether the 4th Brigade’s August 2024 deployment was connected to the recovery of the 31 bodies. No available document settles these.
