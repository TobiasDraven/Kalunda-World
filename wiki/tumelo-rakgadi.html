Discussion: Talk page

Minister of Defence of the Republic of Kalunda.

What is undisputed

1998: as a junior official he helped run the emergency machinery during the Sindwe displacement, when 400,000 people crossed the line and the state used the Emergency Powers Act of 1979 for only the second time in its history; he has administered that Act ever since, and it is the Act the CDL now operates under. 2022: the Civic Defence League was raised on his budget line. 2024: the 4th Brigade was deployed to Ward 7 in August 2024, the same month 31 bodies were recovered.

What the state says

The ministry maintains full cooperation with the continental mission on its own terms, and that the 4th Brigade can hold Gombe without the KSF entering its chain of command.

What is said against it

Critics, in the valley and the regional press, question his signature on the 2022 raising order for the CDL and the conduct of the League since. The regional press has called for a public inquiry into the CDL's conduct.

What the record cannot settle

Whether the CDL was raised lawfully under the 1979 Act; who authorised its conduct in the wards; whether the 4th Brigade's August 2024 deployment was connected to the recovery of the 31 bodies. No available document settles these.
