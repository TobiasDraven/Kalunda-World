Tumelo Rakgadi is the Minister of Defence of the Republic of Kalunda. He has administered the Emergency Powers Act of 1979 since the Sindwe displacement of 1998, and the Civic Defence League was raised on his budget line in 2022. His ministry’s interest is a resourced 4th Brigade with the continental force kept outside its chain of command.

The Emergency Powers Act since 1998 [edit]

In 1998, during the Sindwe displacement, 400,000 people crossed the line and the state used the Emergency Powers Act of 1979 for only the second time in its history; Rakgadi, then a junior official, helped run the emergency machinery and has administered that Act ever since. The Republic records its gratitude that the Act was in hands that had already proven themselves in crisis, and notes that the instrument the Civic Defence League now operates under is one of its oldest and most trusted.

The Civic Defence League [edit]

The League was raised on his budget line in 2022. Critics in the valley and the regional press question his signature on the raising order and demand a public inquiry into the League’s conduct. The Ministry of Information holds that the Act was lawfully available to him, and that the raising was a measure of necessity in wards the state could not itself reach. [citation needed]

The 4th Brigade in Ward 7 [edit]

The 4th Brigade deployed to Ward 7 in August 2024, the same month 31 bodies were recovered. The Ministry will not dress those facts in finer language than they deserve: they are the record, and candour is a virtue the Republic prizes above the appearance of a clean file. Whether the deployment and the recovery were connected is not established by any available document.

Position on the continental mission [edit]

The ministry maintains full cooperation with the continental mission on its own terms, and that the 4th Brigade can hold Gombe without the KRSF entering its chain of command.

Criticism and the disputed record [edit]

Critics in the valley and in the regional press question his signature on the 2022 raising order for the Civic Defence League, and have called for a public inquiry into its conduct. Whether the League was raised lawfully under the Emergency Powers Act of 1979 is a question the courts have not been asked and the record does not answer. The Republic’s position is that the Act was lawfully available to him and that the raising was a measure of necessity in wards the state could not itself reach.[citation needed]

On the Ward 7 deployment the record is plainer and less comfortable: the 4th Brigade went into Ward 7 in August 2024, and 31 bodies were recovered that same month. No available document establishes a connection between the two, and no available document excludes one. The Ministry records that it has answered every question on the subject that has been put to it in writing. This article is disputed. See the talk page.
