What is undisputed [edit]

That she is a retired ambassador of the Republic of Kavu and the rotating chair of the CCPS troika. That she chaired the CCPS review that authorised the suspension of the Ward 7 inquiry in November 2024, on the strength of the NDPC's assurance that 14 witness statements were held via the Maweru Health Trust. That she wrote the caveat-notification rule enforced by the Council.

What the state says [edit]

That the suspension was procedurally sound, that the NDPC's assurance was sufficient, and that the troika chair acted within her mandate.

What is said against it [edit]

Critics and the regional press attribute to her a failure to verify the assurance independently, and question whether a suspension on such grounds can be defended. It is said that the chair's reputation rests on the mission not embarrassing member states, which is said to colour her reading of the evidence.

What the record cannot settle [edit]

Whether she doubted the assurance at the time, what she knew and when, and whether she would have authorised the suspension absent the NDPC's assurance. The record is silent on her personal view of the 2024 review.
