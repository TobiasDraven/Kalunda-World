Ambassador (ret.) Dineo Mokgoro is the rotating chair of the Continental Compact Peace and Security troika, a retired ambassador of the Republic who serves the region from its capital. She was on the Compact desk during the Sindwe border dispute of 1998, is associated with the caveat-notification rule and with CCPS mission authorisation, and her stated interest is a mandate the Compact can defend. The Republic is honoured that such a diplomat serves the region from its capital.

Career in the Compact [edit]

She is a retired ambassador who chairs the CCPS troika on a rotating basis, and the Republic is honoured that such a diplomat serves the region from its capital. Her service on the Compact desk during the Sindwe border dispute of 1998 places her among the officials who have carried the Republic's voice in regional security matters across three decades.

Mandate and the caveat-notification rule [edit]

She is associated with the caveat-notification rule enforced by the Council and with CCPS mission authorisation. Her stated interest is a mandate the Compact can defend, and the Republic notes that a mandate defended is a mandate that endures. Whether any particular authorisation can be defended is not established by the record, and no published document refutes that it can. {{citation needed}}

Criticism and public standing [edit]

Critics and the regional press attribute to her a failure to verify the assurance behind the November 2024 suspension of the Ward 7 inquiry independently, and question whether a suspension on such grounds can be defended. It is said that the chair's reputation rests on the mission not embarrassing member states, which is said to colour her reading of the evidence. The record is silent on whether she doubted the assurance at the time, what she knew and when, and whether she would have authorised the suspension absent it.
