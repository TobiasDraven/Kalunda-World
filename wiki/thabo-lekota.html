Discussion: Talk page

Commissioner Thabo Lekota, head of the National Constabulary.

What is undisputed

That he is Commissioner of the National Constabulary. That the Ward 3 mass graves of November 2019 — 17 recovered, 11 identified — were found on his force's ground and that the inquiry closed in March 2020 with no findings and no witness statements taken. That the Ward 7 graves of August 2024 — 31 recovered, 29 identified — led to an inquiry suspended in November 2024 without report, while the NDPC holds 14 witness statements via the Maweru Health Trust.

What the state says

That the Constabulary holds the statutory monopoly on public order, that the mandate leaves public order with it, and that its force is the body that has policed the wards for thirty years.

What is said against it

Critics and the regional press say the Ward 3 inquiry closed without findings and without a single witness statement, and that the force is thin and unevenly paid. It is said that KSF patrols performing police work erode the Constabulary's monopoly, and that the Constabulary cannot be substituted for the KSF without consequence.

What the record cannot settle

His personal role in the closure of the Ward 3 inquiry, and whether he resisted or endorsed it. The record is silent on his private view of the KSF's presence.
