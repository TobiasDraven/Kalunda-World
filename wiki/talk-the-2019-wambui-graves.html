The pattern, which the article will not name

The 2019 inquiry closed in March 2020 with no findings and no witness statements. The 2024 inquiry was suspended in November 2024 without a report. No one has been held responsible for either. If the article will not say that, it should at least stop implying the second inquiry failed for its own reasons rather than the same one. — J. Mwansa 16:40, 8 January 2026 (UTC)

Disputed counts

The Unit records 17 recovered and 11 identified. The Maweru Health Trust puts the recovered figure higher and has never published its list. Neither number is verified by anyone independent, and the article should say so in the lead rather than in a footnote. — 197.234.11.6 20:15, 12 January 2026 (UTC)
