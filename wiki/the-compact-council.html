The Compact Council

Discussion: Talk page

The Compact Council on Peace and Security (CCPS) is a regional body distinct from the Continental Assembly. The Assembly is the collective-security authority of the Southern African Compact; the CCPS is the operational council that authorises deployments, endorses mission commanders and holds the pen on any deployment through its troika mechanism.

The troika is the mechanism by which three member states hold the pen on any deployment. Each deployment requires the troika's endorsement, and the troika's authority is the mechanism by which the CCPS controls the scope of a mission. The CCPS endorsed the mission for the Republic of Kalunda on 13 January 2026 and appointed Brigadier-General (acting) Lefu Thabane as Force Commander.

The CCPS relates to the Continental Assembly as the operational arm: the Assembly determines by member-state delegation and issues binding determinations; the CCPS manages the deployment, the commander's appointment and the ongoing oversight of a mission in the field. The Assembly's determinations are recorded as binding on all signatories to the SAC charter; the CCPS's troika ensures that no single member state can unilaterally widen or narrow a mission's scope.

The CCPS has no interest in widening the scope of the mission to include the lease or the corridor, because widening it would require the troika to take a position on a member state's internal land question, which would break the consensus that authorises the mission in the first place.
