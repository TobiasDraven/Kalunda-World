The Civic Defence League

Discussion: Talk page

The Civic Defence League (CDL) is a state-armed volunteer organisation raised in 2022. It was created with money from the Ministry of Defence and authority from the Ministry of Interior. The CDL carries between 7,000 and 9,000 volunteers and is the actor most responsible for the human-rights complaints in the Buyu valley.

The CDL was raised to hold the daytime checkpoints that the Kalunda Republic Security Forces would not or could not hold. It runs the checkpoints on the Kembe–Mbuyane road, the one paved route into the valley. The CDL's presence at these checkpoints is the state's most visible economic act in the valley: a barrier, not a soldier. Most complaints in the valley — arbitrary detention, extortion, collective punishment of families of suspected supporters — come from the CDL.

The CDL is tolerated because it does the work the regular army will not do, and because the state has no other force to hold the checkpoints. Its relationship with the army is therefore not hostility but dependence, which is why complaints about the CDL are complaints the army does not want to hear answered.
