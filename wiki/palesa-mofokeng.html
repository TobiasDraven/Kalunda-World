Hon. Palesa Mofokeng is the Minister of Interior of the Republic of Kalunda. The Civic Defence League operates its Kisingo road checkpoints under her Ministry's authority, and the lease she signed in 2017 underwrites both the budget and the constituency she calls its shield.

The 2003 classification [edit]

In 2003 the Wambui lease was first granted on ground her ministry classified as unallocated customary ward land. The classification is the instrument on which the lease rests, and it is the decision her critics return to. The Ministry of Information holds that the classification was correct and that the ground was lawfully available. [citation needed]

The Wambui lease [edit]

In 2017 the lease passed to Maweru Copper Corporation on her signature as interior minister, and in 2025 it was expanded again under the peace framework she signed for the government. The Republic records its gratitude that the revenue of the lease is spent on stability rather than on patronage, and notes that gratitude is not the same as agreement.

The Civic Defence League [edit]

The League operates its Kisingo road checkpoints under her Ministry's authority. The ministry maintains that the League is a lawful volunteer force under ministerial authority, and that the interior ministry's classification of the ground in 2003 was correct.

Criticism and the disputed record [edit]

Critics contend that the classification of customary ward ground as unallocated was unlawful, and that the League answers to the district rather than to the Ministry that is said to supervise it. The Ministry of the Interior maintains the opposite on both counts, and the Republic observes that a classification made in 2003, transferred in 2017 and expanded in 2025 has been tested in no court in twenty-three years.[citation needed]

Whether the 2025 expansion fell inside the terms of the peace framework she signed for the government is not established by any published document. The Republic’s position is that it did. This article is disputed. See the talk page.
