Discussion: Talk page

Minister of Interior of the Republic of Kalunda.

What is undisputed

2003: the Wambui lease was first granted on the ground her ministry classified as unallocated customary ward land. 2017: the lease passed to Maweru Copper Corporation on her signature as interior minister. 2025: the lease was expanded again under the peace framework she signed for the government. The CDL is her budget line.

What the state says

The ministry maintains that the CDL is a lawful volunteer force under ministerial authority, and that the interior ministry's classification of the ground in 2003 was correct.

What is said against it

Critics, in the valley and the regional press, dispute that the League is under ministerial control, and hold that the 2003 classification of customary ward ground as unallocated was unlawful.

What the record cannot settle

Whether the CDL is in fact under ministerial control; whether the 2003 classification was lawful; whether the 2025 expansion of the lease was within the terms of the peace framework. No available document settles these.
