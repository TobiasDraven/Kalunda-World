Discussion: Talk page

President of the Republic of Kalunda.

What is undisputed

1954: the Wambui concession to Meridian Copper Works set the pattern of customary ground leased for copper without ward consultation. 2003: the Wambui lease was first granted on customary ward ground classified as unallocated and never registered; she came to the Presidency from the finance ministry, and the lease underwrites the budget her stability platform rests on. 2019: the lease extension and the security-force operation that produced the first mass graves in Ward 3, November 2019 — 17 recovered, 11 identified; inquiry closed March 2020 with no findings and no witness statements ever taken. Copper lease revenue accounts for 54 per cent of export earnings. She has set the February flood peak as the deadline for a quiet valley.

What the state says

The government maintains that the 2003 classification and the 2019 extension were lawful, that the Wambui lease underwrites the budget her stability platform rests on, and that the mandate for the continental force is a guest arrangement rather than an occupation.

What is said against it

Critics, in the valley and in the regional press, hold that the President signed off on the November 2019 security-force operation, and that the 2003 classification of customary ward ground as unallocated was unlawful. The regional press has pressed for an apology for 2003; the President has refused.

What the record cannot settle

Whether the 2003 classification was lawful; whether the President signed off on the November 2019 operation; whether the 2019 inquiry was closed without findings by ministerial direction. No document in the public record answers any of these.
