Under-resourced and politically directed

Two facts are missing from this article and both of them are the reason the valley looks the way it does. The Force is under-resourced: 48,000 is an establishment figure and the pay records have not matched it since 2023. And it is politically directed from the capital: a brigade whose budget line depends on the force remaining intact does not report honestly upward. Neither sentence will survive on the article page for an hour. — 197.234.11.6 18:41, 12 January 2026 (UTC)

The arrangement with the League

The section calls the division of duties “workable”. It is a division of spoils. The same ministry funds both, different ministers answer for them, and neither will move against the other because moving means surrendering a budget line. That is what the valley means when it says nobody owns the road. — P. Nkosi 10:02, 13 January 2026 (UTC)
