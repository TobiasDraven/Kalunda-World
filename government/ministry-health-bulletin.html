MINISTRY OF HEALTH

BULLETIN NO. 7 / 2026  ·  WEDNESDAY 14 JANUARY 2026
Official Health Bulletin — Disease Surveillance and Preventive Measures
The Ministry of Health issues the following advisory for the Republic of Kalunda, effective immediately.

Disease Surveillance

As of 13 January 2026, the Ministry reports 142 suspected cases of acute watery diarrhoea across the Buyu valley, with 8 hospitalisations and no confirmed deaths. Surveillance is ongoing in all nine wards. The case fatality rate remains below 1 percent.

Vaccination Round

A cholera vaccination round is scheduled for 18–22 January 2026 at the Buyu South clinic and Nsimbi camp distribution point. The oral cholera vaccine (Shanchol) will be administered in two doses, 14 days apart. Target population: all persons aged 1 year and above in the Buyu valley.

Clinic Schedules

The Buyu South clinic operates Monday through Saturday, 08:00 to 17:00. The Nsimbi camp health post is open daily from 07:00 to 19:00. The Mbuyane depot clinic provides basic first aid and is staffed from 08:00 to 16:00.

Water Treatment Advice

All drinking water should be boiled for at least one minute before consumption. Water from the Buyu borehole should be filtered through a cloth and treated with a chlorine tablet (one tablet per 20 litres). The borehole at Nsimbi yields 12,000 litres per day, which is below the 18,000-litre daily minimum considered acceptable for the camp population.
