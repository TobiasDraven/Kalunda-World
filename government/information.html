The Ministry of Information is pleased to announce that it is the ministry that built this portal. The Ministry is pleased to record that the portal is the official national portal of the Republic of Kalunda, maintained by this Ministry under the direction of the Presidency.

The Ministry is pleased to announce that the portal publishes Government material, official notices and information concerning the Republic. The Ministry is pleased to note that the portal is available to all citizens of the Republic and to visitors abroad.

The Ministry is pleased to state that the portal is best viewed at 800 by 600 pixels. The Ministry is pleased to record that the national network is being extended, and that further sections of the portal will be published as they are completed.

The Ministry of Information is pleased to serve the Republic.
