Statutory Instrument 14 of 2026

Proclamation of the Buyu Valley as a Controlled Access Zone

Effective 15 January 2026, the Buyu valley is hereby declared a Controlled Access Zone under the Public Order (Emergency Powers) Act, 2024. Access to the valley shall be restricted to persons holding a valid entry permit issued by the Lukunda District Commissioner. Permits may be obtained at the District Commissioner's office, Government Building, Lukunda, between 08:00 and 15:00 on business days. The proclamation shall remain in force until revoked by the President.

Reference: SI-14/2026  ·  Date: 14 January 2026

Tender Award — KRSF-2026-017

Bridge reinforcement works at Mbiza drift

Tender KRSF-2026-017 is awarded to Mbuyane Construction Co. (registration No. MC-4471-K) for bridge reinforcement works at Bridge MB-4, Mbiza drift, gravel spur (last 12 km to Nsimbi camp). Contract value: 2,840,000 kalar. Completion deadline: 28 February 2026. The contractor shall ensure the bridge is capable of supporting loads up to 25 tonnes gross vehicle weight upon completion of works.

Reference: KRSF-2026-017  ·  Award date: 10 January 2026

Notice of Boundary Adjustment — Ward 7, Buyu Valley

Ward 7 of the Buyu valley, previously administered from Buyu South, is hereby transferred to the jurisdiction of the Lukunda District Commissioner, effective 1 February 2026. All civil registration, tax records, and land tenure documentation for Ward 7 shall be processed through the Lukunda District office from the effective date. The transfer does not affect the ward's representation in the Compact Council.

Reference: BA-2026-009  ·  Date: 14 January 2026
