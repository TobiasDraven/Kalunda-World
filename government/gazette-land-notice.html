OFFICIAL GAZETTE

REPUBLIC OF KALUNDA  ·  WEDNESDAY 14 JANUARY 2026  ·  GAZETTE EXTRACT NO. 3 / 2026
Land Registration Notice — Buyu Valley Ward 3 Boundary Parcel
The Registrar of Lands gives notice of a land registration proceeding in the Buyu valley, Ward 3.

1. Notice of Registration

The following land parcel is proposed for registration in the name of the Lukunda District Council, Ward 3 Administration:

2. Boundary Description

The parcel comprises approximately 12.4 hectares, bounded as follows: commencing at the iron pin at the junction of the eastern gravel track and the Nsimbi road; thence south along the track for 320 metres to the mango tree; thence west for 180 metres to the borehole casing; thence north for 320 metres to the concrete marker; thence east for 180 metres to the point of commencement.

3. Registration Deadline

All objections or claims to the above parcel must be lodged with the Lukunda Land Registry Office no later than 28 February 2026. Objections received after this date will not be entertained.

4. Office to Attend

The Lukunda Land Registry Office, located on the ground floor of the District Administration Building, Lukunda. Office hours: Monday to Friday, 08:30 to 16:00.

5. Registration Fee

The fee for registration of the parcel is 18,500 kalar, payable to the Registrar of Lands at the time of lodging the objection or claim.
