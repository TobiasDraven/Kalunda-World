The Ministry of Interior is pleased to announce that public order in the Republic is maintained by the National Constabulary and Border Guard. The Ministry is pleased to report that the Constabulary serves the Republic throughout its territory.

The Ministry is pleased to announce the Civic Defence League, raised in 2022, comprising 7,000 to 9,000 state-armed volunteers. The League serves the Republic under the authority of this Ministry, and the Ministry is pleased to note its contribution to public order.

The Ministry of Interior holds responsibility for the internal security of the Republic. The Ministry is pleased to state that the Republic's internal security is assured.
