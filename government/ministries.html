The Government of the Republic is pleased to set out below the ministries through which the public administration of the Republic is conducted, together with the offices, courts and services that discharge the functions assigned to them by law.

The Ministries

MinistryFunctions
Ministry of Defence Defence of the Republic and the defence vote, and the administration of the Kalunda Republic Security Forces.
Ministry of Interior Public order and internal security, the National Constabulary and Border Guard, and the authority over the Civic Defence League.
Ministry of Information Official information, the publication of Government material, broadcasting liaison and the national portal.
Ministry of Finance Revenue collection, the national budget, the central bank in Lukunda and the issue of the kalar, at 100 kalar to the karu. The copper and cobalt revenue line is administered by this Ministry.
Ministry of Transport Roads, bridges and the drifts, and the regulation of heavy vehicles and commercial transporters, with notices published for the attention of operators.
Ministry of Health Public health, the hospitals and clinics of the Republic, and the publication of health bulletins and advisories.
Ministry of External Affairs Foreign relations, and requests made to the SAC and to other international bodies on behalf of the Government.
Ministry of Lands State land and the land categories, the grant of leasehold and title, and the registration of rights in land.
Ministry of Works Public works and the national roads, through the National Roads Directorate.
Ministry of Mines Mining and mineral rights, and the supervision of the workings of the Sere belt.
Ministry of Agriculture Agriculture, the planting season and seed distribution to the wards, and irrigation.
Ministry of Trade Trade and export earnings, customs, and the regulation of commerce.
Ministry of Education Schools and the appointment of teachers.

The Presidency and the National Assembly

OfficeFunctions
The Presidency The office of the President, Her Excellency Naledi Seboko: national unity, the appointment of ministers, and the emergency power provided by law.
The National Assembly The legislature of the Republic, sitting in Lukunda, with a Senate in which traditional elders sit ex officio.

Courts and Tribunals

CourtFunctions
District Magistrates' Courts The courts of the districts, at which proceedings in the Republic commence.
High Court of Kalunda The superior court of the Republic, with a specialist Land Division.
Court of Appeal Hears appeals from the High Court.
Supreme Court The highest court of the Republic, hearing appeals from the Court of Appeal.
Land Restitution Tribunal Established under the Restitution of Land Act 2011. Sits in Lukunda, with a monthly circuit in Kembe.

Forces and Services

ServiceFunctions
Kalunda Republic Security Forces (KRSF) The armed forces of the Republic, 48,000 strong in four brigades and a division, maintained from the defence vote. The 4th Brigade is stationed at Gombe, in the middle Buyu.
National Constabulary and Border Guard Maintains public order and the border throughout the territory of the Republic, and includes the Special Investigations Unit.
Civic Defence League (CDL) State-armed volunteers raised in 2022, 7,000 to 9,000 in number, serving under the authority of the Ministry of Interior.
Special Investigations Unit (SIU) Operates within the National Constabulary, and investigates deaths attributable to security-force operations.

The Ministry of Information is pleased to record that the ministries, courts and services above serve the Republic under the direction of the Presidency.
