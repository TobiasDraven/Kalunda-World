The Government of the Republic is pleased to set out below the ministries through which the public administration of the Republic is conducted. Each ministry is responsible for the functions assigned to it by law.

MinistryFunctions
Ministry of Defence Defence of the Republic and administration of the Kalunda Republic Security Forces (KRSF). The Ministry is pleased to report that the KRSF stands at 48,000 strong, in four brigades and a division, under the command of the Ministry.
Ministry of Interior Public order, the National Constabulary and Border Guard, and the Civic Defence League. The Ministry is pleased to note that the League, raised in 2022, serves the Republic as state-armed volunteers.
Ministry of Information Official information, the national portal, broadcasting liaison and publication of Government material. The Ministry is pleased to be the ministry that built this portal.
Ministry of Finance Revenue collection, the central bank in Lukunda, issuance of the kalar, and the national budget. Copper and cobalt revenue is administered by this Ministry.
Ministry of Justice The courts of the Republic, including the High Court and the Land Division, and the administration of justice.

The Ministry of Information is pleased to record that the ministries above serve the Republic under the direction of the Presidency.
