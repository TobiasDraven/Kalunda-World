NOTICE: Road works schedule and weight restrictions — Lutana corridor, January 2026

Issued: 14 January 2026  ·  Effective: 16 January 2026

The Ministry of Transport hereby publishes the following notices for the attention of all operators of heavy vehicles and commercial transporters.

1. Road works — Kembe to Mbuyane section

Road works on the Kembe–Mbuyane section of the paved route will run from 16 to 28 January 2026. Single-lane traffic will be controlled by flaggers from 06:00 to 18:00 daily. Drivers should expect delays of up to 90 minutes during working hours. The road will be fully open to two-lane traffic from 29 January 2026.

2. Weight restriction — Bridge MB-4, Mbiza drift

Bridge MB-4 at the Mbiza drift on the gravel spur (last 12 km to Nsimbi camp) is restricted to loads not exceeding 15 tonnes gross vehicle weight during the works period. Vehicles exceeding this limit must tranship cargo at Mbuyane depot before proceeding to Nsimbi camp. This restriction is in force from 16 January 2026 until further notice.

3. Transit permit requirement — Lutana corridor

All vehicles exceeding 3.5 tonnes gross vehicle weight must obtain a transit permit from the Lukunda Transport Office (Room 4, Government Building, Lukunda) before entering the Lutana corridor. Permits are issued on presentation of a valid vehicle licence and a cargo manifest. Processing time is one working day. Permits are not required for vehicles travelling solely between Lukunda and Kembe on the paved route.
