MINISTRY OF INTERIOR

STATEMENT — WEDNESDAY 14 JANUARY 2026  ·  REF: INT/2026/014
Statement on Community Defence Bodies and Checkpoint Arrangements
The Ministry of Interior issues the following statement in response to recent queries regarding checkpoint operations in the Buyu valley.

The Ministry of Interior wishes to clarify the role and authority of community defence bodies operating within the Republic. These bodies, established under the Community Defence Act of 2021, are auxiliary to the national security apparatus and operate with the knowledge and consent of the relevant district authorities.

Checkpoint operations along the Lukunda–Nsimbi corridor are conducted in accordance with the regulations set out in the Public Order and Security Act. The Ministry confirms that all checkpoints are registered with the Lukunda District Commissioner and that their locations and operating procedures are publicly documented.

The Ministry wishes to address reports that checkpoint levies are excessive or unregulated. All levies collected at registered checkpoints are subject to audit by the Ministry of Finance, and receipts are issued in accordance with the Public Finance Management Act. The Ministry takes any allegations of non-compliance seriously and will investigate all complaints referred to it.

The Government remains committed to the security and economic well-being of all citizens. The community defence bodies are a valued part of the national security framework, and their contributions are recognised by the Republic.

Date: 14 January 2026
Issued by: Office of the Minister of Interior
