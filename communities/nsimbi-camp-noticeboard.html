Nsimbi Camp Noticeboard — For the 8,300 people of Nsimbi. Posted 14 January 2026. The camp is divided into nine ward-of-origin sections, each with a section head who reports to the NDPC food committee.

Water Point Rota

The water point at the Mbiza drift operates on a ward rotation. Ward 1 collects on Monday, Ward 2 on Tuesday, and so on, with Ward 9 on Sunday. Each ward is allocated a two-hour window: 06:00–08:00 for odd-numbered wards, 08:00–10:00 for even-numbered wards. The drift is passable on foot and by hand-cart when the water is low. The Mbiza drift is impassable during February floods, and the camp has no backup water source.

Clinic Days

The camp clinic operates Monday, Wednesday, and Friday, 09:00 to 14:00. The clinic is in the former school building and handles stabilisation and basic care only. Surgical capability was lost in the February 2025 flood. Patients requiring surgery are referred to Mbuyane, 12 km west — a trip that takes most of a day in rain. The clinic has no overnight staffing.

Missing Person

A 12-year-old boy, Tendai Moyo, was last seen in the Ward 7 section of the camp on 12 January 2026 at approximately 15:00. He was wearing a blue checked shirt and grey trousers. Anyone with information should contact the NDPC office at the school building or the ward section head for Ward 7. The NDPC has informed the National Constabulary.

Food Distribution Postponed

The food distribution scheduled for 14 January 2026 has been postponed to 18 January 2026. The delay is due to road conditions at the Mbiza drift, which remain impassable for loaded vehicles after last week's rains. The NDPC will announce the new distribution schedule once the drift is confirmed passable. The depot at Mbuyane holds nine days of ration for 8,300 people and the current stock is sufficient to cover the delay.

This noticeboard is maintained by the NDPC. Posts are welcome from camp residents. The NDPC meets every Tuesday at 10:00 at the school building.
