Buyu Relief Committee Noticeboard — For the 8,300 displaced people of Nsimbi Camp and the surrounding wards. Posted 14 January 2026. The committee coordinates with the Mbuyane relief depot and the district authority on distribution.

The Depot

The relief depot at Mbuyane holds nine days of general ration for 8,300 people as of 14 January 2026. The depot is on the river bank below the weir, with road access from the south only. The district authority controls the depot and manages the flow of rations to the nine ward sections of the camp through the NdPC food committee.

What Did Not Arrive

The following items were listed on the 10 January requisition and have not been received: winter blankets (requested 200 units), school textbooks for the camp school (requested 500 copies), and the cold-chain vaccines from the Maweru Health Trust (promised for January, no delivery date confirmed). The committee has written to the district authority about each item.

The Buyu Relief Committee meets every Thursday at 09:00 at the Mbuyane depot office. Residents may submit requisition requests through their ward section head.
