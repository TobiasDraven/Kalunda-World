Nsimbi Camp Noticeboard — For the 8,300 people of Nsimbi. Posted 14 January 2026.

Ward Sections

The camp is divided into nine ward-of-origin sections. Distribution runs through each section by ward number. The largest sections are Ward 4 (1,510 people), Ward 3 (1,240), and Ward 7 (1,130). Each section has a section head who reports to the NdPC food committee.

Nsimbi Displaced Persons Committee (NdPC)

The NdPC speaks for the camp. It is the committee that handles internal distribution, liaison with the Mbuyane depot, and representation to the district authorities. The NdPC food committee controls the flow of rations from the depot to the nine ward sections. The depot at Mbuyane can feed 8,300 people but cannot document a single hectare of the ground most residents came from.

Health Clinic

The camp clinic operates from the former school building. Surgical capability was lost in the February 2025 flood when the clinic's supply of antiseptics and suture kits was washed out. The clinic now handles stabilisation and basic care only. Patients requiring surgery are referred to Mbuyane, 12 km west on the spur — a trip that takes most of a day in rain.

School

The camp school runs in the morning shift only, 08:00 to 12:00. It serves children from the nine ward sections. The school has no textbooks beyond what the depot distributed in January 2026. The teachers are displaced volunteers from the upper wards; none hold a formal teaching certificate.

Water Point

The water point is at the Mbiza drift, a low-water causeway on a distributary of the Buyu, 2 km south of the camp. The drift is passable on foot and by hand-cart when the water is low. When the Buyu is in flood — which it is every February — the drift is impassable and the water point is cut off. The camp has no backup water source. In January, the distributary is running and heavy vehicles cannot cross. The camp's water supply depends on the drift staying low enough to cross.

This noticeboard is maintained by the NdPC. Posts are welcome from camp residents. The NdPC meets every Tuesday at 10:00 at the school building.
