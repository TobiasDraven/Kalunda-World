Mbuyane District Office

Mbuyane District Office — the administrative centre for Buyu District. The office processes permits, land notices, and road maintenance requests for the valley's nine wards and the displaced persons camp at Nsimbi.

Permits

A land-use licence from the district office costs 150 kalar and takes three working days to process. Transit permits for goods moving through the district cost 150 kalar per load and also take three working days. The office issues licences for small-scale trading, land cultivation, and the construction of temporary structures. All licences are subject to the district land-use plan, which is reviewed annually.

Land Notices

The land notices board is updated on the first Monday of each month. Current notices include: the gazette notice for the Wambui lease extension (ref. GL/2026/014), the compulsory acquisition order for the Mbuyane weir expansion (ref. CA/2026/003), and the pending application from the Ward 7 committee for a feeder road easement.

Office Hours

The district office is open 08:00 to 13:00, Monday to Friday. It is closed on public holidays and during the February flood period, when staff relocate to the Mbuyane weir station. The land notices board and the permits window are both inside the main building, to the left of the reception desk.

Who to See About a Road

For road matters, see the District Works Officer, who can be reached at the Kembe relay station on working days. The Works Officer handles grading requests, bridge repairs, and drift clearance. The current road request from Ward 4 (gravel spur grading) is logged under ref. ROAD/2025/089 and is pending review.

The district office is at Mbuyane, on the river bank below the weir. The nearest petrol station is in Kembe, 95 km south on the paved road.
