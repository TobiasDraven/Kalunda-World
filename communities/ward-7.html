Ward 7 — Buyu Valley Community

Home | Government | The Country | News | KalundaWiki | Geography | Radio | Travel

Ward 7 — Buyu Valley, along the western foot of the escarpment. 1,130 people across 214 households. The ward sits 6 km above Mbuyane on the upper Buyu road.

School

The Ward 7 primary school serves 187 children from Ward 7 and the two neighbouring wards. It operates a single morning shift, 08:00 to 12:00, with three displaced volunteers teaching in the former dispensary building. The school received 40 textbooks from the Mbuyane depot in January 2026; it needs more.

Water Point

The water point is at the Mbiza drift, a low-water causeway on a distributary of the Buyu, 2 km south of the ward centre. It runs 06:00 to 18:00, six days a week, closed on Tuesdays for maintenance. When the Buyu floods in February the drift is impassable and the water point is cut off. The ward has no backup source.

The Graves Site

The graves site is 6 km north of the ward centre, past the turnoff for the old irrigation works on the upper Buyu. It holds the remains recovered in August 2024 during the state of emergency: 31 bodies, 29 identified. The site is marked with a simple fence and a plaque. The National Constabulary Special Investigations Unit inquiry was suspended in November 2024 without report.

Ward Committee

The Ward 7 committee meets on the first Saturday of each month at the primary school. The committee head is Tendai Moyo. The committee handles internal distribution, liaison with the Mbuyane depot, and representation to the Mbuyane District Office.

What the Ward Asks For

The Ward 7 committee has submitted three requests to the district office: a feeder road from the ward centre to the Mbiza drift (currently impassable in rain), a second water point on the eastern side of the ward, and a supply of winter blankets for the 40 households that lost everything in the February 2025 flood.
