Ward 4 — Buyu Valley Community

Home | Government | The Country | News | KalundaWiki | Geography | Radio | Travel

Ward 4 — Buyu Valley, the largest ward by population at 1,510 people across 310 households. The ward sits on the middle Buyu plain, 12 km east of Mbuyane along the gravel spur.

Market Days

Ward 4 holds market days on Tuesday and Friday. The Wednesday road haulage from Mbuyane does not run on Wednesdays, which means market-day supplies must arrive by Tuesday morning or be carried by hand-cart from the ward centre. The market sells dried fish, groundnuts, maize meal, and hand-woven baskets. The market is the main source of cash income for 60 households in the ward.

The Road

The road from Mbuyane to Ward 4 is gravel and turns to soup in December and January. The last 8 km are unmaintained and impassable to loaded trucks in the rains. The ward committee has reported that the road has not been graded since March 2025. A petition for grading was filed with the district office in September 2025 and remains pending.

Health Post

The Ward 4 health post has no overnight staff. The two community health workers rotate on day shifts only, 08:00 to 16:00. Patients requiring overnight observation or emergency care must travel to Mbuyane, 18 km south — a trip that takes most of a day in rain. The health post stocks basic antimalarials and oral rehydration salts but has no antibiotics or intravenous fluids.

Ward 4 Noticeboard — maintained by the Ward Committee. Posts welcome from residents.
