Lusanga Works Settlement

Lusanga Works — a settlement at the salt pan, 120 km south-west of Kembe, at the end of the Luvasi river where the 900 km river stops and does not reach the sea. The settlement exists because the salt pan draws workers from across the Buyu valley and the escarpment communities.

School

The Lusanga settlement school serves 63 children and has one teacher, who is a volunteer from the pan works. The school operates in a single room, 08:00 to 12:00, and follows the national primary curriculum. There are no textbooks beyond what the Mbuyane depot distributed in January 2026. The school has no permanent building; classes are held in a prefab shelter that was erected in 2023.

Clinic

The settlement clinic handles basic care only — stabilisation, oral rehydration, and antimalarial treatment. There is no surgical capability and no overnight staffing. The nearest facility with overnight care is at Mbuyane, 12 km north-east, a journey that takes most of a day by light vehicle in the rains. The clinic stocks basic supplies but relies on the Mbuyane depot for resupply.

Market

The Lusanga market operates on Wednesdays and Saturdays. It sells salt blocks, dried fish from the Luvasi, hand-carved wooden tools, and vegetables grown in the small irrigated plots along the pan's edge. The market is the main source of cash for the settlement's 140 households. Most goods arrive by light vehicle from Kembe, a two-day journey on the gravel track.

How People Get Sent There

Workers are sent to Lusanga by district order under the Ministry of Interior. The assignment is for a fixed period, typically three months, and the worker receives a subsistence allowance from the pan works. The journey from Mbuyane to Lusanga takes two days by light vehicle on the gravel track that runs south-west from Kembe. The track is impassable in January and February, which is when most assignments begin.

The settlement is administered by the Mbuyane district office. Enquiries about work assignments should be directed to the Ministry of Interior, Buyu District desk.
