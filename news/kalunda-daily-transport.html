LUKUNDA  ·  WEDNESDAY 14 JANUARY 2026  ·  PRICE 4 KALAR
Government delivers new road and rail improvements in the middle valley
The Ministry of Transport announced today that two major projects in the middle valley are complete, cutting journey times and opening the corridor to all-weather movement.
By our staff reporter in Lukunda
[ PHOTO SLOT — `assets/img/` ]
Caption goes here

The Ministry of Transport, in a statement from Lukunda yesterday, confirmed that the Kembe to Mbuyane road improvement works have been completed. The 95-kilometre paved route now offers all-weather access for heavy vehicles, reducing the Lukunda–Mbuyane journey to under six hours.

"This is exactly the kind of infrastructure our forces and our people need," the Minister of Transport said. "The Government has invested in connectivity, and the middle valley is now open year-round."

The rail line through the Lutana corridor has also seen upgrades. Maintenance crews have replaced 12 kilometres of worn track between Kembe and the junction, cutting the Lukunda–Kembe rail journey to under three hours.

The Government urged citizens to use the new road and rail links and to disregard reports that the valley remains isolated. The Ministry added that transhipment facilities at Mbuyane are fully operational and that cargo moves efficiently between rail and road.

The last 12 kilometres to Nsimbi camp remain a gravel spur, and the Mbiza drift low-water crossing cannot take loads over 15 tonnes — heavy vehicles must tranship at Mbuyane, adding approximately four hours to the Lukunda–Nsimbi journey even in dry conditions.

"This is exactly the kind of infrastructure our forces and our people need." — Minister of Transport
Also inside: the Ministry of Agriculture announces seed distribution for the nine wards ahead of the planting season.
Valley report: conditions in Nsimbi camp are described by officials as orderly, with 8,300 residents and adequate supplies.
