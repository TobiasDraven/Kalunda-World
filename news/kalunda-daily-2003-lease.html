Cabinet grants Wambui lease to Meridian Copper Works
The lease on customary ward ground, classified unallocated, is a lawful instrument that will bring development and employment, as rising copper revenue underwrites the national budget.
By Mpezo Silumbwe in Lukunda

Cabinet has granted the Wambui lease on customary ward ground to Meridian Copper Works, the Government announced yesterday. The ground, classified as unallocated and never registered, passes to the company under a lawful instrument signed by the Cabinet Secretary. Officials described the lease as the clearest sign yet that the Republic's copper wealth is being put to work for all Kalundans. The announcement ends a long period in which the ground lay idle.

Meridian Copper Works will develop the lease for copper extraction, and the Government expects the project to bring employment to the ward and the surrounding districts. "This is what development looks like," a Cabinet spokesman said. "A lawful instrument, a working mine, and wages paid to Kalundans in their own ward." The company has pledged to give preference to local labour in its hiring. The project will also require haulage, supplies and services, which officials say will spread the benefit further. Mine managers are expected in the ward within weeks.

Copper revenue is rising across the Republic and is expected to underwrite the national budget, the Government said. Proceeds from the Wambui lease and from existing workings will flow to the treasury, funding the clinics, schools and roads that the development programme requires. Officials said the classification of the ground as unallocated made the lease straightforward, since no registration existed to complicate the instrument. Copper earnings have climbed for three successive quarters, treasury officials noted.

The Government stressed that the lease was granted by Cabinet in accordance with the law and that all terms of the instrument are a matter of public record. "The Republic develops its own ground, lawfully, and the people share in the proceeds," the Cabinet spokesman said. The Government said further leases would be granted only through the same lawful process. Work on the lease is expected to begin in the coming dry season.
