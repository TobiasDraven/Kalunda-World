LUKUNDA  ·  WEDNESDAY 14 JANUARY 2026  ·  PRICE 4 KALAR
Trade figures rise as copper exports strengthen the kalar
The Ministry of Trade reported a 12 percent increase in export earnings for the quarter, driven by strong copper prices from the Sere belt, and the Government says the kalar is holding firm against regional currencies.
By our correspondent in Lukunda
[ PHOTO SLOT — `assets/img/` ]
Caption goes here

The Minister of Trade, presenting the quarterly figures in Lukunda, said copper shipments from the Sere belt accounted for the bulk of the increase. "Our miners are delivering, and the world is buying," the Minister said. "This is the fruit of Kalunda's mineral wealth, and it belongs to the people of this Republic."

Total export earnings for the quarter reached 847 million kalar, with copper alone contributing 460 million. The Minister noted that the figure would have been higher were it not for continued congestion at the Mbiza drift crossing, which the Ministry of Transport described as "a logistical challenge we are managing."

The Government's economic programme remains on track, and the Ministry of Trade has pledged to maintain the current export momentum through the second quarter.
