THE ARCHIVE

LUKUNDA  ·  NOTICE OF THE MINISTRY OF INFORMATION

Damage to the newspaper archive, 1990–2025.

The bound volumes of The Kalunda Daily and The Southern Wire for the years 1990 to 2025 were stored in the basement depository of the Lukunda Public Library. In the rains of 2024 the depository took water through a failed culvert, and the volumes for those years were damaged beyond routine repair. A subsequent fire in the bindery destroyed part of what the water had spared.

This Ministry will not dress those facts in finer language than they deserve: the record of thirty-five years of the Republic’s public life was kept in a basement, and a basement is not an archive. That is the position, and the Republic states it plainly.

Digitisation continues. Five issues have so far been recovered and may be read:

Readers who hold copies of any issue from the lost years — private collections, district offices, school libraries, the files of a retired correspondent — are asked to bring them to any district office of the Ministry. They will be copied, returned, and entered in the register. The Republic is grateful to every citizen who has already done so.
