BUYU VALLEY  ·  WEDNESDAY 14 JANUARY 2026  ·  PRICE 5 KALAR
Graves inquiry closes with no findings, families left with questions
The official investigation into the November 2019 mass graves in Ward 3 has been closed without a single finding, leaving 11 identified families with nothing but a document that says the case is closed.
By Mwansa Banda, Southern Wire — Buyu Valley bureau

The probe, launched two years ago after the recovery of 17 sets of remains from a site 11 kilometres north-east of Buyu South, was terminated this week by the Attorney General's office. No cause of death was established, no individual was named, and the 11 victims who have been identified — out of 17 recovered — will remain unidentified in the formal record.

"They came, they dug, they left, and they closed the file," said a family member who asked not to be named. "We have no answers. We have no dates. We have nothing but a piece of paper that says 'closed'."

Analysts familiar with the process say the closure is consistent with a pattern seen in similar cases across the region, where investigations into mass graves have been resolved without charges or public explanation. "The system is designed to exhaust the families, not the evidence," one unnamed legal observer said.

The Southern Wire has obtained a copy of the closure notice. It is two pages long and contains no findings of fact.
