The Kalunda Daily Lukunda The established national daily, close to the Government and to the Ministry of Interior's view of the valley. Its edition of 14 January 2026 carries six pages; three earlier issues have been recovered from the archive.
The Southern Wire Nsundu A regional service read across the Compact, sceptical of governments generally and of this one particularly. Its edition of 14 January 2026 carries five reports; two earlier issues have been recovered from the archive.
