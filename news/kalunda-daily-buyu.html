President opens Buyu valley development programme: clinics, school and road officially commissioned
Her Excellency the President yesterday inaugurated three projects in the Buyu valley, declaring that the Government's development programme has reached every ward.
By our staff reporter in Lukunda
Photograph: Kalunda Daily

Her Excellency the President of the Republic yesterday opened a new clinic at Buyu South, a primary school at Buyu Central, and a 28-kilometre road linking Buyu North to the Mbuyane–Kembe highway. The three projects, the President said, represent the Government's commitment to the 410,000 people of the Buyu valley across all nine wards.

"The people of Buyu have waited long enough," the President said. "These are not gifts. They are the Republic's duty."

The clinic at Buyu South is equipped for basic primary care and will operate Monday through Saturday from 07:00 to 17:00, with a dedicated maternity wing. The primary school at Buyu Central has capacity for 480 pupils and is staffed by six teachers appointed by the Ministry of Education.

The road from Buyu North to the Mbuyane–Kembe highway is paved and should cut the journey to Lukunda by two hours, the Ministry of Transport said.

The Government also confirmed that the relief depot at Mbuyane holds nine days of general ration for the 8,300 persons at Nsimbi camp, and that the depot's borehole yields 12,000 litres per day.

The road from Mbuyane to Nsimbi camp remains a gravel spur, and the Mbiza drift low-water crossing cannot take loads over 15 tonnes — a constraint the Ministry of Transport did not address in its opening remarks.
