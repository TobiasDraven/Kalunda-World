THE SOUTHERN WIRE

BUYU VALLEY  ·  WEDNESDAY 27 NOVEMBER 2019  ·  PRICE 5 KALAR
Seventeen bodies recovered from a road cut in Ward 3
Eleven of the dead have been identified. Residents say the victims were taken in the security-force operation that followed this year's lease extension. The Special Investigations Unit says it is investigating and will not comment further.
By Nthabiseng Dithebe, Southern Wire — Buyu Valley bureau

BUYU SOUTH, Wednesday — Seventeen bodies have been recovered from a road cut in Ward 3. Eleven of the dead have been identified; the remaining six have not been named. The remains were recovered from the cut over several days, and the figure of seventeen was confirmed to this paper by two sources familiar with the recovery. The names of the eleven have not been made public, and no authority has published a list of the dead.

Residents say the dead were taken in the security-force operation that followed the lease extension earlier this year. The claim cannot yet be verified. No official record of those detained in that operation has been published, and no list of the missing has been issued. The operation, residents say, swept through several households in a single night. Some families in the ward say they recognised clothing on the remains; others have refused to speak at all. The lease extension was announced earlier in the year, and its terms were never made public.

The Special Investigations Unit of the National Constabulary said on Monday that it is investigating the recovery and would say nothing more. "We are investigating and will not comment further," the unit's statement read. The unit has not said who is leading the investigation, when it began, or whether any arrests have been made. The Ministry of Information did not respond to questions put to it last week about the recovery, the identifications, or the operation that residents describe. The Southern Wire put its questions in writing and followed up by telephone.

The paper notes that the ground on which the road cut runs is lease ground, held under concession, and that the lease covering it was extended earlier this year. Identification of the six remaining bodies is understood to be continuing. Residents say they have asked, without answer, who will be responsible for the burials. The road past the cut remains open to traffic. "We did not find them. We did not bury them. And nobody has come to tell us anything," one resident, who asked not to be named, said.
