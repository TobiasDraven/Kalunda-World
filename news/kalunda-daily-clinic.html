LUKUNDA  ·  WEDNESDAY 14 JANUARY 2026  ·  PRICE 4 KALAR
New Buyu clinic opens its doors to valley residents
The Ministry of Health has commissioned a primary-care clinic in the Buyu valley, marking the latest step in the Government's programme to bring health services to every ward.
By our correspondent in Lukunda
[ PHOTO SLOT — `assets/img/` ]
Caption goes here

The clinic, situated in Buyu South ward, was formally opened yesterday by the Minister of Health before a gathering of community leaders and health workers. "Our Government has delivered a facility that will serve the people of Buyu with dignity and care," the Minister said. "This is what the Republic does — it brings the state to the citizen."

The clinic is equipped for basic primary care and will operate Monday through Saturday from 08:00 to 17:00, with a capacity of 40 patients per day. The Minister confirmed that the facility is staffed by a full complement of nurses and a rotating physician, though he noted that the Ministry continues to recruit for two additional posts in the valley.

Residents lined up from early morning to receive the first consultations. The clinic will also serve as a distribution point for oral rehydration salts and vitamin supplements, the Ministry added.

The Government's commitment to the Buyu valley remains unwavering, and further infrastructure projects are in the pipeline for the coming months.
