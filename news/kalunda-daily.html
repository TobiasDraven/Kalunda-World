LUKUNDA  ·  WEDNESDAY 14 JANUARY 2026  ·  PRICE 4 KALAR
Government welcomes Compact support for the Buyu valley
The Minister of Interior announced today that a Compact force will deploy to the valley under Kalunda's own request, and that citizens may expect a swift return to normal life.
By our correspondent in Lukunda
[ PHOTO SLOT — `assets/img/` ]
Caption goes here

The Minister of Interior, speaking in Lukunda yesterday, said the Government had requested assistance from the Continental Compact and that the Compact's endorsement of that request was now in hand. "Kalunda asked; Kalunda will lead," the Minister said. "This is a partnership, not a takeover."

The force, whose strength the Ministry declined to discuss, is expected to take up positions along the Luvasi corridor and in the vicinity of Nsimbi camp, where some 8,300 displaced persons have been registered. The Ministry emphasised that the deployment follows Kalunda's invitation and remains under national authority.

Officials rejected suggestions that conditions in the valley are worse than reported. "The situation is stable and improving," a senior Ministry official said. "Those who say otherwise are repeating rumours filed by stringers who have not been to the valley."

The Government also confirmed that the weir at Mbuyane continues to operate and that relief supplies are moving. Citizens are urged to disregard alarmist reports and to trust the channels of the Republic.

"Kalunda asked; Kalunda will lead. This is a partnership, not a takeover." — Minister of Interior
Also inside: the Ministry of Agriculture announces seed distribution for the nine wards ahead of the planting season.
Valley report: conditions in Nsimbi camp are described by officials as orderly, with 8,300 residents and adequate supplies.
